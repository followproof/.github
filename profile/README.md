# Influencer audience check by Followproof

Influencer audit and influencer vetting for Instagram, TikTok and YouTube. Name a creator and Followproof checks audience quality from public data, runs an engagement rate check against accounts of the same size and estimates what a sponsored post is worth, so creator due diligence is done before you pay.

## Connect with AI assistants

Our [MCP server](https://github.com/followproof/mcp-server) connects Followproof to compatible AI clients, with browser sign-in and explicit permission.

get_account reads your plan and usage. check_creator, get_creator_report and compare_creators score Instagram, TikTok and YouTube creators with the signals behind the score, engagement against same-size accounts and rate estimates. search_creators finds creators by niche, size and country. list_lists, get_list, create_list, add_to_list and remove_from_list manage shortlists. Uses your plan's allowance; nothing posts or spends money.
